# RC-000113 — Atlas Digital Asset Payments
Supports BTC, XRP, ETH, HBAR, ADA, and XLM as invoice payment rails. Payment intents are invoice/tenant/workspace bound; network and asset are explicit; quotes expire; amounts use atomic integer units; reconciliation detects wrong asset/network and under/overpayment. XRP destination tags and XLM memos are policy-enforced. Receiving authority is separated from REFUND/WITHDRAW/CONVERT treasury authority. No private keys belong in ordinary application state; signing/custody must remain isolated behind governed providers/HSM/MPC/custody infrastructure before production enablement.
